One word describing an ex: **podcaster**.

My ex is a podcaster. That is the whole verdict — the podcast
nobody asked for, recorded in a closet, three listeners, two of
them his mum's phones. The joke arrives with the word itself:
no insult, no caption, just an occupation that says everything
about who he was. Funny at a glance, surprising because it is not
the obvious first pick, and anyone who has dated a podcaster
recognises it instantly.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `podcaster` (9 characters, a single word, no trailing newline)
to `/output/entry.txt`. Standard library only.
